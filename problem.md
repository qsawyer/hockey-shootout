# Problem

People who enjoy hockey and are looking for a quick game to play that doesn't take too much commitment.

# Initial requirements

* R1 The player chooses where to shoot, one of the corners, 5 hole, or under the left or right arm.
* R2 The goalie picks 2 thirds of the net to cover, either choosing horizontally or vertically.
* R3 If the goalie chose sections that cover where the shot was its a save otherwise its a goal.
* R4 Each side gets 5 shots
* R5 If the score is tied after each side shoots 5 times, it goes to sudden death, alternating each player taking one shot.
* R6 show the score and the round before and after every shot
* R7 Display if it was a goal or a save
* R8 Display the winner at the end
* R9 Prompt user to play again
